Behavior In This Zone: In this regime, the default behavior is mean reversion, pinning, and move suppression. The natural center is 80,000, and the first reflex is usually a pullback toward that axis.

Behavior In Positive Gamma: In positive gamma (72,000-82,000), price action is more controlled, more mean-reverting, and more likely to be dampened by hedge flow.

Transition Zone: The transition zone sits around 72,000. As long as spot stays above it, the pin logic remains intact; acceptance below it starts to shift the market from absorbing moves to amplifying them.

Behavior In Negative Gamma: The negative-gamma / hedge-acceleration zones sit lower at 77,500 and 77,000. If those pockets open up, dealer flow can amplify downside.

Where To Look For Shorts?: Band icinde short ancak 82,000 civari reject veya 72,000 alti acceptance ile aranir. Band ortasinda yone kovalamak weak trade olur.

Where To Look For Longs?: Inside the band, longs become cleaner on holding above 72,000-80,000 or on acceptance above 82,000.

No-Trade / Unconfirmed Zone: Spot 72,000-82,000 araliginda sikişiyorsa and akis karisiksa en weak trade ortadan zorla yone girmektir.

Base Case: Base case: gamma rejimi destekleyici. Spot 72,000 ustunde and denge bandinin icinde kaldikca ana beklenti kontrollu trade, pin and yukari denemelerde once 82,000 civarinda surtunme.
