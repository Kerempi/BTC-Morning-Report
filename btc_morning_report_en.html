Behavior In Positive Gamma: In positive gamma (64,000-65,000), price action is more controlled, more mean-reverting, and more likely to be dampened by hedge flow.

Transition Zone: The transition zone sits around 64,000. As long as spot stays above it, the pin logic remains intact; acceptance below it starts to shift the market from absorbing moves to amplifying them.

Behavior In Negative Gamma: The negative-gamma / hedge-acceleration zones sit lower at 60,000 and 55,000. If those pockets open up, dealer flow can amplify downside.

Where To Look For Shorts?: Band icinde short ancak 70,000 civari reject veya 64,000 alti acceptance ile aranir. Band ortasinda yone kovalamak weak trade olur.

Where To Look For Longs?: Inside the band, longs become cleaner on holding above 64,000-66,000 or on acceptance above 70,000.

No-Trade / Unconfirmed Zone: Spot 64,000-70,000 araliginda sikişiyorsa and akis karisiksa en weak trade ortadan zorla yone girmektir.

Base Case: Base case: gamma rejimi destekleyici. Spot 64,000 ustunde and denge bandinin icinde kaldikca ana beklenti kontrollu trade, pin and yukari denemelerde once 65,000 civarinda surtunme.
