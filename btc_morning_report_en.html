Behavior In This Zone: In this regime, the default behavior is mean reversion, pinning, and move suppression. The natural center is 65,000, and the first reflex is usually a pullback toward that axis.

Behavior In Positive Gamma: In positive gamma (62,500-63,000), price action is more controlled, more mean-reverting, and more likely to be dampened by hedge flow.

Transition Zone: The transition zone sits around 62,500. As long as spot stays above it, the pin logic remains intact; acceptance below it starts to shift the market from absorbing moves to amplifying them.

Behavior In Negative Gamma: The negative-gamma / hedge-acceleration zones sit lower at 60,000 and 58,000. If those pockets open up, dealer flow can amplify downside.

Where To Look For Longs?: Inside the band, longs become cleaner on holding above 62,500-65,000 or on acceptance above 68,000.
